# Acknowledgments

Nexent was never the work of one person. From the first line of code to the platform it is today — deployable in minutes and forged in real production scenarios — there has always been a group of people behind it, each investing time and care at a different stage.

This file exists to remember two kinds of contribution:

1. **Code Contributors** — team members who helped build Nexent's core codebase and have since moved on to new chapters;
2. **Special Thanks** — partners who never pushed code, but whose real-world perspective and candid advice profoundly shaped where the platform went.

One of the meanings of open source is that every contribution gets seen, and remembered.
